# Refund & Returns Policy — ready to paste

Paste the section below into **Shopify admin → Settings → Policies → Refund
policy**. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value first.

This is written to be as protective as US consumer law actually allows. It is not
legal advice and has not been reviewed by an attorney — have one look at it
before you take real orders, especially if you sell outside the US.

**Why it is shaped this way** — the two rules that constrain it:

- **FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (16 CFR Part 435).**
  You must have a reasonable basis to ship within your stated time, or within 30
  days if you state none. If you can't, you must offer the buyer the choice to
  cancel for a full refund. That right cannot be contracted away, and refunds owed
  under it must be sent within **7 working days**. This is why cancellation is
  tied to *dispatch* rather than a fixed number of days: a "no cancellation for
  20 days" term would collide with this rule on every delayed order.
- **Chargeback rights cannot be waived.** The Fair Credit Billing Act and
  Electronic Fund Transfer Act give them, and terms restricting them are
  unenforceable. A customer who is refused a reasonable cancellation disputes the
  charge instead — you lose the money *and* take the chargeback. Excessive
  chargebacks cost you the merchant account, which is a far bigger loss than any
  refund.

The policy below still protects you properly: **used items are non-returnable,
the customer pays return shipping, and orders cannot be cancelled once
dispatched.** Those are all enforceable.

## StrideWell Refund & Returns Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

### Return window

You may request a return within **30 days of delivery**. Requests made after 30
days cannot be accepted.

### Condition of returned items

StrideWell sells foot and leg products, which are **personal hygiene items**. To
be eligible for a return, an item must be:

- unused, unworn and untried;
- in its original packaging, with all tags, inserts and protective film intact;
- with any hygiene seal unbroken.

**Items that have been worn, used or tried on cannot be returned or refunded.**
This includes insoles that have been placed inside footwear, socks and compression
garments removed from their sealed packaging, and braces or supports that have
been fitted. This is a hygiene restriction and we cannot make exceptions.

### Items that cannot be returned

- Any used, worn or tried-on item, as set out above
- Items with a broken hygiene seal or missing original packaging
- Items marked **Final Sale** or **Clearance** at the time of purchase
- Gift cards

### Items that are always covered

Regardless of the conditions above, contact us and we will replace the item or
refund you in full, including shipping, if your order arrives:

- damaged in transit;
- defective or faulty;
- not the item you ordered.

Please contact us within **14 days of delivery** for these cases and include
photographs of the item and its packaging so we can resolve it quickly.

### How to start a return

Email **[SUPPORT@YOURDOMAIN.COM]** with your order number and the reason for the
return. We will confirm whether the item is eligible and, if it is, send you a
return authorisation and the return address.

**Do not send an item back before receiving a return authorisation.** Unauthorised
returns cannot be tracked to your order and may not be refunded.

### Return shipping

**You are responsible for return shipping costs** unless the item was damaged,
defective or incorrect. We recommend a tracked service — we cannot refund an item
that does not reach us.

Original shipping charges are non-refundable except where the item was damaged,
defective or incorrect.

### Refunds

Once your return arrives and has been inspected, we will email you to confirm
whether it has been approved.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within **[5] business
days** of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take a further 5–10 business days
to post the funds — that part is outside our control.

If a return is rejected because the item was used, worn or is not in its original
condition, we will contact you. You may have it returned to you at your cost, or
we can dispose of it.

### Order changes and cancellations

**Orders can be cancelled or changed at any time before they are dispatched.**
Email us as soon as possible with your order number. We process orders quickly, so
please contact us straight away.

**Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled.** Tracking is emailed
at dispatch. If you no longer want the item after that point, wait for it to
arrive and request a return under the terms above.

### Delivery delays

If we cannot dispatch your order within the time indicated when you placed it, we
will contact you with a revised estimate and you may choose to **cancel your order
for a full refund** instead of accepting the delay.

### Address changes

We can only change a delivery address before dispatch. Once an order has shipped
the address cannot be altered. Orders returned to us as undeliverable because of
an incorrect address supplied at checkout will be refunded less the original
shipping cost.

## Optional clauses

Add these only if you want them, and understand the trade-off.

### Restocking fee

> A restocking fee of **[15]%** is deducted from refunds on returned items that
> are not damaged, defective or incorrect.

Legal in most states **if disclosed before purchase**. It also reliably annoys
customers and increases the chance they dispute the charge instead of accepting
the partial refund. For a store with no reviews and no track record, the goodwill
is usually worth more than the fee.

### Shorter window

You can reduce 30 days to 14. Note that **California** requires a conspicuously
posted policy — if a retailer doesn't have one, the buyer effectively gets 30 days
to return for a refund. 30 days is also what most customers now expect, and a
shorter window is a visible negative next to competitors.

## What to do after publishing

Once this is live in Settings → Policies, three things in the theme can be
switched on — they are deliberately off while no policy exists:

1. **Returns FAQ row** — replace the "Not sure which one to order?" row on the
   homepage, collection and product templates with a returns answer linking to
   `/policies/refund-policy`.
2. **Fourth trust tile** — swap "Ships to All 50 States" for "30-Day Returns"
   with the `return-arrow` icon.
3. **Policy band section** — available in the theme editor, on no template by
   default.

Do not switch these on before the policy is published. See COMPLIANCE.md.
