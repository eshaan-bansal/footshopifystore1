StrideWell Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Return window

You may request a return within 30 days of delivery. Requests made after 30 days cannot be accepted.

Condition of returned items

StrideWell sells foot and leg products, which are personal hygiene items. To be eligible for a return, an item must be:

  • unused, unworn and untried;
  • in its original packaging, with all tags, inserts and protective film intact;
  • with any hygiene seal unbroken.

Items that have been worn, used or tried on cannot be returned or refunded. This includes insoles that have been placed inside footwear, socks and compression garments removed from their sealed packaging, and braces or supports that have been fitted. This is a hygiene restriction and we cannot make exceptions.

Items that cannot be returned

  • Any used, worn or tried-on item, as set out above
  • Items with a broken hygiene seal or missing original packaging
  • Items marked Final Sale or Clearance at the time of purchase
  • Gift cards

Items that are always covered

Regardless of the conditions above, contact us and we will replace the item or refund you in full, including shipping, if your order arrives:

  • damaged in transit;
  • defective or faulty;
  • not the item you ordered.

Please contact us within 14 days of delivery for these cases and include photographs of the item and its packaging so we can resolve it quickly.

How to start a return

Email [SUPPORT@YOURDOMAIN.COM] with your order number and the reason for the return. We will confirm whether the item is eligible and, if it is, send you a return authorisation and the return address.

Do not send an item back before receiving a return authorisation. Unauthorised returns cannot be tracked to your order and may not be refunded.

Return shipping

You are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item was damaged, defective or incorrect. We recommend a tracked service — we cannot refund an item that does not reach us.

Original shipping charges are non-refundable except where the item was damaged, defective or incorrect.

Refunds

Once your return arrives and has been inspected, we will email you to confirm whether it has been approved.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within [5] business days of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take a further 5–10 business days to post the funds — that part is outside our control.

If a return is rejected because the item was used, worn or is not in its original condition, we will contact you. You may have it returned to you at your cost, or we can dispose of it.

Order changes and cancellations

Orders can be cancelled or changed at any time before they are dispatched. Email us as soon as possible with your order number. We process orders quickly, so please contact us straight away.

Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled. Tracking is emailed at dispatch. If you no longer want the item after that point, wait for it to arrive and request a return under the terms above.

Delivery delays

If we cannot dispatch your order within the time indicated when you placed it, we will contact you with a revised estimate and you may choose to cancel your order for a full refund instead of accepting the delay.

Address changes

We can only change a delivery address before dispatch. Once an order has shipped the address cannot be altered. Orders returned to us as undeliverable because of an incorrect address supplied at checkout will be refunded less the original shipping cost.
