# Privacy Policy — ready to paste

Paste into **Shopify admin → Settings → Policies → Privacy policy**. Replace every
`[BRACKETED]` value.

Not legal advice, and not reviewed by an attorney. It is written against the
**CCPA/CPRA baseline**, which is the practical way to cover the twenty US state
privacy laws in force through 2026 — they differ in detail but converge on the
same obligations. If you sell into the EU or UK, GDPR adds requirements this does
not cover.

**Two things this policy assumes you will actually do:**

1. **Enable Shopify's cookie banner and consent tracking** — Settings → Customer
   privacy. The policy below says you honour opt-outs and Global Privacy Control.
   That is only true if consent tracking is switched on.
2. **Keep the ad-platform list accurate.** Running a Meta, TikTok or Google Ads
   pixel is what makes you a "seller/sharer" of personal information under most
   state laws. If you add a pixel later, this policy has to be updated.

## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

[YOUR BUSINESS NAME] ("StrideWell", "we", "us") operates [YOURDOMAIN.COM]. This
policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we
share it with, and the choices you have.

### Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- Contact details — name, email address, phone number
- Shipping and billing addresses
- Order details, including what you bought and any notes you send us
- Correspondence, when you email us or use a contact form
- Marketing preferences, if you subscribe to our emails

We do **not** collect or store your full payment card details. Payments are
processed by Shopify Payments and other payment providers, who receive your card
information directly.

**Information collected automatically**

- Device and browser type, operating system, language
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Pages viewed, products viewed, referring site, and how you move through the
  store
- Cookie and similar identifiers — see *Cookies and tracking* below

**Information from third parties**

- Order and delivery status from our fulfilment partners and carriers
- Fraud-screening signals from our payment providers
- Aggregated advertising and analytics data from platforms we use

### Why we use it

- To process, fulfil and deliver your orders, and to send order and delivery
  updates
- To answer your questions and provide customer support
- To detect and prevent fraud and abuse
- To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations
- To improve our products, site and service
- To send marketing emails **where you have asked to receive them**, and only
  until you unsubscribe

### Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as follows:

- **Shopify**, which hosts this store and processes orders on our behalf
- **Payment providers**, to take payment and screen for fraud
- **Suppliers and fulfilment partners**, who receive the name, address and order
  contents needed to pack and ship your order. Some are located outside the
  United States.
- **Carriers**, to deliver your order and provide tracking
- **Analytics and advertising providers** — currently [LIST THEM, e.g. Google
  Analytics, Meta, TikTok, or write "none"]. Under several US state privacy laws,
  sharing data with advertising platforms for targeted advertising counts as
  "sharing" or a "sale" even when no money changes hands. See *Your choices*.
- **Professional advisers and authorities**, where we are legally required to
  disclose information

### Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart working, remember your
preferences, understand how the store is used, and — where enabled — to measure
and target advertising.

You can manage non-essential cookies through the cookie banner on this site, and
through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will stop the cart and
checkout from working.

**Global Privacy Control.** If your browser or an extension sends a Global Privacy
Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sale or
sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising, in the states
where that right applies.

### Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **know** what personal information we hold about you and how we use it;
- **access** a copy of it;
- **correct** inaccurate information;
- **delete** your information;
- **port** your information to another service;
- **opt out** of the sale or sharing of your information, and of targeted
  advertising;
- **not be discriminated against** for exercising any of these rights.

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states with
comprehensive privacy laws have these rights by statute. We extend the same
choices to **all** customers regardless of location.

**To exercise any right**, email **[PRIVACY@YOURDOMAIN.COM]** with the request and
the email address used on your order. We will verify who you are before acting,
and respond within the timeframe the applicable law requires — generally 45 days.
You may use an authorised agent.

**To opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information**, email us at the
address above, use the cookie banner, or send a GPC signal from your browser.

### Marketing emails

We send marketing email only to people who have asked for it. Every marketing
email includes an unsubscribe link and our postal address, and we act on
unsubscribes promptly. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop transactional
messages about an order you have placed.

### Sensitive information

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, health information, government
identifiers, or other sensitive categories of personal data. Our products are
consumer comfort items, not medical devices, and we do not need medical details to
sell them to you.

### How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed to fulfil the order and then for as long
as tax, accounting and legal obligations require — generally seven years. Support
correspondence is kept for [24] months. Marketing contacts are kept until you
unsubscribe.

### Security

We use industry-standard measures, including TLS encryption in transit, and rely
on Shopify's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure for payments. No method of
transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

### International transfers

### Children

This store is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect
their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information,
email us and we will delete it.

## After publishing

- Enable **Settings → Customer privacy → Cookie banner** and consent tracking, and
  set region rules. The GPC and opt-out statements above are only accurate once
  this is on.
- If you add an advertising pixel, come back and update the third-party list.
- If you later collect SMS numbers, this policy needs an SMS section and you need
  separate express written consent under the TCPA — that is a different and
  stricter standard than email.
