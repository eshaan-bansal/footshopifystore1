# Shipping Policy — ready to paste

Paste into **Shopify admin → Settings → Policies → Shipping policy**. Replace every
`[BRACKETED]` value.

Not legal advice. Two things about this one specifically:

**It states no delivery window, deliberately.** Stock is dropshipped, so transit
time varies by item and supplier. The FTC's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order
Merchandise Rule holds you to whatever time you advertise, or **30 days** if you
advertise none — and if you miss it you must offer cancellation and a refund. A
policy promising "5–8 business days" would put you in breach on every slow order.
The storefront copy already matches this: it says delivery times vary and that
tracking is emailed on dispatch.

**It discloses that items ship from multiple locations, including outside the
US.** This is the disclosure dropshipping stores most often omit, and omitting it
is exactly what FTC actions against dropshippers have turned on — buyers expecting
domestic delivery and getting weeks of international transit. Saying it plainly
costs you very little and removes the single most common complaint and chargeback
reason.

## Shipping Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

### Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states, including PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses where the
carrier allows. [We do not currently ship internationally. / We also ship to
[COUNTRIES].]

### Shipping costs

- **Free standard shipping on US orders over $50.**
- Orders under $50: a flat rate of **$[X.XX]**.
- Any expedited options and their costs are shown at checkout.

Shipping is calculated and displayed at checkout before you pay. There are no
additional handling or service fees.

### Processing and dispatch

Orders are processed on business days, excluding weekends and US public holidays.
Most orders are dispatched within a few business days of being placed.

**We do not guarantee a dispatch or delivery date.** Our range is fulfilled by
several suppliers and processing time varies by item.

### Delivery times

**Delivery times vary by item.** Different products ship from different locations,
including warehouses **outside the United States**, so transit time depends on what
you ordered and where it ships from. Some items arrive in a few days; others take
considerably longer.

We will not quote a delivery window we cannot hold to. What we commit to instead:

- you receive a **tracking link by email as soon as your order ships**;
- if we cannot dispatch within the time indicated when you ordered, we will
  contact you with a revised estimate and you may **cancel for a full refund**
  rather than accept the delay.

If you need an item by a particular date, email us before ordering and we will
tell you honestly whether it is realistic.

### Orders with multiple items

Because items ship from different locations, **an order containing several items
may arrive in separate parcels on different days**, each with its own tracking.
This is normal and does not mean anything is missing. If part of your order has
not arrived within a reasonable time of the rest, contact us.

### Tracking

Tracking is emailed to you at dispatch. It can take 24–48 hours for a carrier's
system to show the first scan. If tracking has not updated for an extended period,
contact us and we will chase it with the carrier.

### Delivery addresses

Please check your address carefully at checkout. **We can only change an address
before dispatch.**

Orders returned to us as undeliverable because of an incorrect or incomplete
address will be refunded less the original shipping cost.

### Delays outside our control

Carrier backlogs, weather, customs inspection, strikes and other events outside
our control can delay delivery. We will help you track and resolve any delayed
parcel, but we cannot control carrier timescales.

### Lost, stolen or damaged parcels

- **Marked delivered but missing** — check with neighbours and around your
  property, then contact us. We will open an investigation with the carrier.
- **Lost in transit** — contact us if tracking has not moved for [10] business
  days and we will investigate and replace or refund your order.
- **Arrived damaged** — contact us within **14 days of delivery** with photos of
  the item and packaging, and we will replace it or refund you in full.

### Customs and import charges

[If you ship internationally:] International orders may attract duties, taxes or
customs charges on arrival. These are set by the destination country, are not
included in our prices, and are the recipient's responsibility. We cannot predict
them and cannot refund an order refused because of them.

### Cancellations

You may cancel before dispatch — email us as soon as possible with your order
number. Once dispatched, an order cannot be cancelled; see our Refund Policy for
returns.
